Backup and checkpoints

Before Phase 1, the whole project — including work that is not committed yet — is saved as a recovery baseline and proven restorable by restoring it into a temporary folder. After every successful phase a new checkpoint is saved the same way. Checkpoints live in .vibe-to-engineering/checkpoints.git, separate from the project's own history, which is never changed.

If something breaks

Work stops. The broken state is saved, compared with the last good checkpoint, and the cause is found before anything else happens. You then choose between the proposed fix, putting the project back to the last good checkpoint, or stopping. Nothing is ever rolled back without your approval.
